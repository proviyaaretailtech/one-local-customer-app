# Swiggy study: visual system (UI specialist)

Scope: how things look — header, service tabs, search, chips, cards, badges, prices, floating cart, tab bar, headings, density, icons. We borrow Swiggy's patterns only. We keep One Local's own lime/forest brand and glass surfaces.

Paths are relative to `frontend/`. Line numbers match the repo as it stood when this was written.

---

## The main finding in one paragraph

Our look already has more character than Swiggy's: the glass, the colour for each department, the motion. Our problem is **legibility and how much useful information we show**. Much of the important text is 7–10 pt. The discount badge is 7 pt, tab labels 9 pt, the search placeholder 10 pt, the delivery address 10 pt, the section subtitles 10 pt and the checkout note 8 pt. Swiggy never goes below about 11 pt for anything that matters. It puts price, rating, time and offer on every card. We show only name, weight and price, and we don't show rating or time even though `Product` already carries both (`src/api.ts:68-72`). Most items below fix that and don't touch the brand.

## Top 10 changes, ranked by impact vs effort

### 1. Add a type scale with an 11 pt floor — Effort S (highest impact per hour)
- **Swiggy:** body and meta text is about 12–13 pt. Small caps labels ("FREE DELIVERY", "70% OFF") are 11 pt, heavy, with letter spacing. Headings are 17–18 pt and bold. Nothing a customer must read is tiny.
- **Us:** `theme.ts` has spacing and radius tokens but **no type tokens** (`src/theme.ts:143-160`), so every file picks its own sizes. Examples:
  - discount badge 7 pt (`src/components/product-card.tsx:28`, `discountText`)
  - tab labels 9 pt (`src/components/bottom-navigation.tsx:31`)
  - department labels 9 pt (`src/components/department-strip.tsx:28`)
  - address 10 pt (`src/components/app-shell.tsx:104`)
  - search placeholder 10 pt (`app-shell.tsx:106`)
  - section subtitle 10 pt (`src/components/catalog-sections.tsx:50`)
  - filter chips 10 pt (`src/components/department-screen.tsx:33`)
  - floating-cart savings 9 pt (`src/components/floating-cart.tsx:42`)
  - checkout note 8 pt (`app/(tabs)/cart.tsx:387`)
  - ADD label 10 pt (`product-card.tsx:28`)
- **Change:** add the `type` tokens below (see the token delta) and move these usages onto them.
  - Rule: `micro` (11 pt) is the smallest size for anything a customer must read.
  - Mapping: discount → `badge`; tab and department labels → `micro` at 600 weight; address → `caption`; placeholder → `body`; subtitle → `caption`; chips → `label`; ADD → `button`.
- **Why first:** it touches every screen, carries no risk, and is the biggest single "looks professional" gain.

### 2. Product card: put the offer on the photo, the ADD button on the photo's edge, and show rating and time — Effort M
- **Swiggy:**
  - The offer is bold white capitals on a dark gradient at the bottom-left of the photo.
  - The ADD button is a white pill that overlaps the bottom edge of the photo, with the price in brand colour.
  - A meta line reads "★ 4.3 (1.2K) · 20–25 mins".
- **Us:**
  - The offer is a 7 pt glass chip (`product-card.tsx:21`, `s.discount`).
  - ADD is a full-width 44 pt row *below* the price (`product-card.tsx:24`, `s.add`). It adds about 50 pt of height to every card, so fewer cards fit on screen.
  - There is no rating or time, although `p.rating` and `p.delivery_min` exist.
- **Change** (in `ProductCard`):
  - **Offer:** add a bottom scrim over the image: `LinearGradient` with colours `[colors.scrimClear, colors.scrimInk]`, 44 pt tall. Put `{discount_pct}% OFF` on it in `type.badge`, white, letter spacing 0.4.
  - **ADD:** make it `position: absolute` with `right: 6` and `bottom: -16`, anchored to `imageWrap`.
    - Size 34×72 pt, radius `radius.xs` + 4 (10), background `colors.surface`, 1 pt border in `palette.color`, text `type.button` in `palette.color`.
    - Add `hitSlop={{ top: 6, bottom: 6, left: 4, right: 4 }}` so the tap target stays at least 44 pt.
    - The stepper takes the same position and footprint.
  - **Meta line:** under the name, `★ {rating} · {delivery_min} min` in `type.caption`. The star uses `colors.rating`.
  - **Footer:** price row only. Price is `type.price` (15/800); MRP is `type.caption` with strikethrough. Remove the `s.footer` gap.
  - **Result:** card height drops from about 230 pt to about 190 pt, and each card shows more.

### 3. Veg / non-veg mark on every food item — Effort S for the UI, but it needs a data field
- **Swiggy:** a small square with a dot sits before every dish name, on the restaurant page and in the cart. Indian food customers expect this, and FSSAI labelling uses the same symbol.
- **Us:** there is no mark anywhere. A diet preference exists (`src/customer.tsx:11`, `Diet = '' | 'veg' | 'egg' | 'nonveg'`), but `Product` has no diet field (`src/api.ts:68-72`).
- **Change:**
  - Add a new component `src/components/diet-mark.tsx`, `<DietMark diet="veg"|"egg"|"nonveg" size={12} />`.
  - Drawing: a square 12×12 with a 1.5 pt border and radius 2.
    - veg: green border with a green dot (6 pt)
    - non-veg: a brown/red triangle
    - egg: a yellow dot
  - Colours come from the new `diet*` tokens.
  - Show it in the food product card before the name, in cart rows (`cart.tsx:181`), and on the store page.
- **Needs:** `diet?: 'veg'|'egg'|'nonveg'` on `Product`. This is a backend or schema decision, so I'm flagging it to the data agent. Until the field exists, show nothing, never a guess.

### 4. A store (restaurant) list row — Effort M
- **Swiggy:** the core list is one row per restaurant.
  - Left: a large rounded photo (about 112 pt) with an offer overlay ("FLAT DEAL 50% OFF") and a heart.
  - Right, top to bottom: name (16/700), a green rating pill with count · time, cuisines, locality · distance, and a "FREE DELIVERY" line.
- **Us:** stores appear only as small cream capsules: a 42 pt logo plus "20 min · 4.5 rating" at 10 pt (`app/(tabs)/index.tsx:53`, styles `:62-63`). There is no row format for listing places, and a city marketplace needs one for restaurants, pharmacies and shops.
- **Change:** add a new component `src/components/store-row.tsx`, `<StoreRow store offer? distanceKm? locality? freeDelivery? />`.
  - **Photo:** 112×124, radius `radius.md` (22). Offer scrim as in item 2. Heart top-right using 44 pt hit area.
  - **Text column:**
    - name `type.title` (16/700)
    - `<RatingPill>` + " · 25–30 min" in `type.body` 600
    - cuisines or tags `type.caption` muted, 1 line
    - "Locality · 1.2 km" `type.caption` muted
    - optional "Free delivery" in `type.badge`, `colors.onSuccess`
  - **Layout:** padding 16 horizontal and 12 vertical; hairline divider `colors.divider` between rows.
  - Use it on Food, on Care, and on the "Meet your neighbourhood" section, as a vertical list instead of the capsule rail.

### 5. A small badge family: rating pill, deal price tag, "Applied" chip — Effort S
- **Swiggy:** three small components do most of the "reason to buy" work.
  - A **green rating pill** (white star and number).
  - A **yellow price tag** for deal prices.
  - A **"✓ Applied"** state on savings.
- **Us:**
  - Rating is a plain star in a pastel-yellow chip (`app/store/[id].tsx:133-136`) or 10 pt text (`index.tsx:53`).
  - There is no deal tag.
  - "Applied" is a whole green row (`cart.tsx:205-209`). That works, but it isn't a reusable component.
- **Change:** add a new component `src/components/badges.tsx`.
  - `RatingPill({ value, count? })`
    - Height 20, `paddingHorizontal: 6`, radius `radius.xs` (6).
    - Background `colors.ratingBg` (forest), or `colors.ratingLowBg` below 3.5.
    - Text white `type.badge`, with a star icon at size 10.
    - Optional count after it in `type.caption` muted, e.g. "(1.2K)".
  - `DealTag({ price })`
    - Background `colors.dealBg` (electric lime `#B3F400`), text `colors.dealInk` (forestDeep), `type.price`.
    - Radius 6, with a notch (a 6 pt circle cut-out on the left using `colors.surface`).
    - This is our lime version of Swiggy's yellow tag. Use it for "Under ₹99" items (`index.tsx:54`) and future deal rails.
  - `AppliedChip({ label })`
    - Height 24, background `colors.success`, text `colors.onSuccess` `type.badge`, with a `checkmark-circle` icon at 12.
  - `OfferRibbon({ text })`: the image-scrim text from items 2 and 4.

### 6. Header: put the location first, and rotate the search placeholder — Effort S–M
- **Swiggy:** the top-left of the header is where you are.
  - A bold saved label ("Home ›") at about 17 pt, with the one-line address under it at 12 pt, truncated.
  - The search placeholder rotates through real queries ("Search for 'Cake'") with a vertical roll animation. The mic sits inside the bar.
- **Us:**
  - The logo takes the first line. The location is a 10 pt line tucked under it with `marginTop: -5` (`app-shell.tsx:74-77`, style `:104`).
  - The placeholder is a fixed tagline at 10 pt, "A little search. A lovely find." (`app-shell.tsx:78`, `:106`). It doesn't say what can be searched.
- **Change:**
  - **Location:** turn it into a two-line block and put it on the left of the top row.
    - Line 1: `{label ?? city}` in `type.title` (16/800) with a `chevron-down` (12).
    - Line 2: the full address in `type.caption`, `numberOfLines={1}`.
    - Move the brand wordmark down to the compact header, or shrink it to the `OneGlyph` (28) at the right, next to the wallet, cart and profile buttons.
  - **Search:** new `RotatingPlaceholder` inside `searchRow`, fed from the department list:
    - `Search "biryani"` → `"atta"` → `"earphones"` → `"sunscreen"` → `"movie tickets"`
    - 2.6 s per item; 220 ms translateY roll of 14 pt plus opacity.
    - Respect `useMotionAllowed()`: when motion is reduced, show a static "Search food, groceries, shops…" (the compact header already uses this line, `app-shell.tsx:89`).
    - Text `type.body`, colour `colors.muted`. Search bar height 47 → 48.
  - Keep our separate mic and camera buttons. Photo search is something Swiggy doesn't have.

### 7. Department strip as "folder tabs" that merge into the page — Effort M
- **Swiggy:** the service tabs are tall rounded tabs with 3D icons. The **selected tab is filled with the page colour and joins the white sheet below it**, so you can see which world you are in. Service promises ("10 mins") sit on the tab as a small pill.
- **Us:**
  - Five icons on the gradient. The selected one gets a glass square plus a 12×3 dot (`department-strip.tsx:17`, `:28`).
  - Labels are 9 pt. There is no service-promise pill.
  - Our pages already start with a sheet that has 34 pt top corners (`index.tsx:61`, `department-screen.tsx:33`), so a joined tab would work naturally.
- **Change:**
  - **Selected item:**
    - Background `colors.glassBright` (the same colour as the sheet), `borderTopLeftRadius`/`borderTopRightRadius: 18`, bottom radius 0.
    - `paddingBottom` 8 more, and `marginBottom: -1` so it touches the sheet.
    - Drop the dot.
  - **Labels:** `type.micro` at 600 weight, 700 when selected, `numberOfLines={1}`.
  - **Service pill:** add an optional `promise` field to `departments.ts` (e.g. Grocery "15 min", Book It "Tonight"). Draw it at the top-right of the icon:
    - height 16, `paddingHorizontal: 5`, radius 8
    - background `colors.forestDeep`, text `colors.electricLime` 10/800
  - Only show a promise we can keep in the current city. Otherwise leave it out.

### 8. Sticky chips, and a bottom bar that hides while scrolling — Effort M
- **Swiggy:**
  - On scroll, the header collapses and the search and chip rows stick to the top.
  - The bottom tab bar slides away while scrolling down, and a small floating "Search" pill appears.
  - The restaurant page's filter chips stick too.
- **Us:**
  - The compact search morph already exists and is good (`app-shell.tsx:82-92`).
  - The filter chips do not stick, on the department page (`department-screen.tsx:25`) or the store page (`app/store/[id].tsx:167-191`, commented "sticky-styled" but it isn't sticky).
  - The tab bar is always visible. The floating cart then sits 84 pt above it (`app-shell.tsx:94`), so on a phone about 150 pt of the bottom is permanently covered.
- **Change:**
  - **Chips:** make the chip row a direct child of the `ScrollView` and add `stickyHeaderIndices`. On the store page, the chip header is child index 2. The chip row gets `backgroundColor: colors.surface` with a hairline bottom border once stuck.
  - **Tab bar:**
    - In `BottomNavigation`, read `useCityScroll().y`. When scrolling down, `translateY` the bar to 110; when scrolling up, bring it back to 0. Use a 220 ms timing, and apply it only on the scrolling routes.
    - The FloatingCart `bottom` animates from `insets+84` to `insets+12` along with it.
    - Chip text becomes `type.label`. Chip height stays 36 on the store page and 44 elsewhere.
    - The active chip on the store page changes from solid black `brandPrimary` to `colors.forestDeep` with white text, so it matches the cart tabs (`cart.tsx:332`).

### 9. Cart: savings at the top, strikethrough on every item, outlined stepper — Effort S
- **Swiggy:**
  - A green strip at the very top: "✧ ₹86 saved!"
  - Each item shows its old price struck through next to the new one.
  - The stepper is outlined in brand colour on white.
  - The savings section lists each benefit with "✓ Applied".
- **Us:**

  - Item rows show only the price (`cart.tsx:183`), although `mrp` is on the item.
  - The stepper is solid black (`cart.tsx:365-368`), which looks different from the palette stepper on product cards.
- **Change:**

    - Height 36, background `colors.success`, text `type.label` 700 in `colors.onSuccess`, `sparkles` icon 14.
    - Copy: "₹{n} saved on this order".
  - **Item rows:** `₹{price}` in `type.price`, followed by `₹{mrp}` in `type.caption` muted with strikethrough when `mrp > price`.
  - **Stepper:** background `colors.surface`, 1 pt border `colors.forest`, glyphs and quantity in `colors.forest` 800, radius 10, height 36 with `hitSlop` to 44.
  - **Free-delivery "unlocked" row** (`cart.tsx:169-172`): render the new `AppliedChip` on the right instead of the icon.
  - Leave the pay bar to the payments work. Only one visual note: `checkoutMeta` 8 pt → `type.micro`.

### 10. Store page: overlapping info card, green rating pill with count, offer pager, "Menu" jump button — Effort M–L
- **Swiggy:**
  - The info card overlaps the dark header and holds: name, "20–25 mins | locality ▾", a rating pill with "10K+ ratings", and a rotating offer strip ("BUY 1 GET 1 FREE", 1/5 pager).

- **Us:**

  - **Offer strip:** add `<OfferPager offers />` under the card.

  - **Sections:** group products by `category_id` into sections with a `SectionTitle` and a count.
  - **Menu button:** a floating button at bottom-right.

## What we already do better than Swiggy (keep these)

1. **Floating cart with a free-delivery progress bar and item thumbnails.** The product image also flies into the bar (`floating-cart.tsx:29-35`). Swiggy's bar is flatter and doesn't show progress.
2. **The server-quoted bill.** It explains GST (CGST/SGST breakdown), shows the struck-through delivery fee as FREE, and offers a "smart top-up" to reach free delivery (`cart.tsx:147-167`, `:250-283`). It is clearer and more honest than Swiggy's bill.
3. **A colour world for each department** (`paletteFor`, gradients such as `foodTop/Mid/Fade`). It carries a sense of place that Swiggy's one-colour style lacks. Item 7 makes it more visible.
4. **Accessibility.**
   - Tap targets are 44 pt almost everywhere, including the heart, stepper, chips and tabs.
   - Reduced motion is respected everywhere (`useMotionAllowed`).
   - Screen-reader labels and states are used throughout.
   - Swiggy's chips and toggles are often smaller than 44 pt.
5. **Photo and voice search** next to the search bar (`app-shell.tsx:78`). Swiggy has voice only.
6. **Compact search header morph** on scroll (`app-shell.tsx:82-92`): this is already Swiggy-level.
7. **No ad clutter.** There are no "AD" tags, no stacked membership upsell ribbons, and no magenta promo pills competing with the content. We get Swiggy's density from item 1–2 information, not from promotions.
8. **Honest sample labelling** ("Sample order · no charge", catalogue notices). Keep it until real stores are live, but at `type.micro` or above, not 8–9 pt.

## Gaps and risks (for the lead)

- Item 3 (veg mark) needs a `diet` field on products. This is a data/schema decision; the UI must not guess.
- The item 7 promises ("15 min") and the item 4 distance or free-delivery lines must come from real per-city data. With national coverage, a false promise costs more than showing none.
- Instamart, Dineout and Scenes weren't captured, so there is no visual input for Grocery-in-depth or Book It from Swiggy.
- The type-scale change (item 1) touches many files. Do it as one sweep after the payments build merges, to avoid conflicts.
