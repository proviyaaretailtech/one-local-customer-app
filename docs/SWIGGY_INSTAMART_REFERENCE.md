# Swiggy Instamart — interaction reference (read 2026-09-24)

Read from the founder's own Swiggy app over iPhone Mirroring, viewing only (no taps that
order, pay or edit the cart). Patterns to learn from, not screens to copy: One Local keeps
its own visual design.

## Home
- Header: delivery time ("18 mins") + saved address, profile top-right.
- Department tabs as illustrated cards (Food / Instamart / Dineout / Scenes).
- Search box whose placeholder rotates through real products ("Search for 'Ice Cubes'",
  then "'Power Bank'"), with a list/scan icon and a saved-items icon beside it.
- Offers carousel, then a row of round category shortcuts.
- **Floating free-delivery bar above the tab bar**: "Add ₹64 more to unlock FREE DELIVERY",
  a progress line, and "CART · 1 ITEM" with the last item's thumbnail and a chevron.
- Tabs: Instamart · Categories · Reorder · Print.

## Categories tab
- "Shop by Store" row of arch-topped store cards (Print, Book, The Noice, Sports & Fitness).
- Then grouped sections (Fresh items, Grocery & Kitchen, …), each a **4-column grid** of
  pastel tiles: product-photo composition on top, two-line label below.

## Category listing (e.g. Fresh Vegetables)
- Back + title + search in the header.
- **Left rail** of sub-categories, each an image + two-line label; selected one highlighted.
- **Filter chips** across the top: filter icon · Sort By · Price Drop · Brand.
- 2-column product grid. Each card:
  - image with a save (bookmark) icon top-right and a **small square "+" add button
    overlapping the image's bottom-right corner** (not a full-width ADD bar),
  - veg/non-veg mark, **pack-size chips** (250 g · 2×250 g · +2),
  - price + struck MRP, **unit price** ("₹6/100 g"), "% OFF" in green,
  - name (two lines max) and delivery time ("18 MINS").

## Product page
- Opens as a **card sliding up over the dimmed listing** (close chevron, save, share).
- Image carousel with page dots; "18 MINS" badge on the image.
- "Explore all Fruits & Vegetables Category items >" link back into the category.
- Name, one-line description, **type/variant chips**, then **quantity cards** — each with its
  own "% OFF", price, struck MRP and unit price (1 kg · 3×1 kg · 3 kg).
- **Sticky bottom bar**: "Potato · 1 kg · 22% OFF", price, big ADD button.
- Under the card: **a strip of round thumbnails of neighbouring products** — swipe to move to
  the next product without going back to the list.

## Cart
- Address header; **Cart / saver-membership toggle**; savings banner ("₹4 saved!").
- Unavailable items grouped at the top with **Replace**, **Move to wishlist** and delete.
- "Savings corner" → Apply Coupon.
- **"Add GSTIN — claim GST credit up to 18%"** for business buyers.
- Recommendation tabs: "Did you forget?" · "Stock up essentials" · seasonal.
- Sticky primary action at the bottom that explains what is blocking checkout.
- (Bill details are hidden while the cart has no available items, so they were not read.
  One Local's bill follows the founder's notebook rules instead: MRP-inclusive GST for
  packaged goods, 5% GST (2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST) on top for restaurant food.)
