39. Core Strategic Model
PROVIYAA should operate as a Commerce Operating System rather than a POS with a marketplace attached.
BUILD ONCE     ↓ CORE COMMERCE OS     ↓ ┌────────────┬────────────┬────────────┐ VINII POS    ONE LATUR    ONLATUR     ↓            ↓            ↓ MERCHANT      CUSTOMER      DELIVERY     └────────────┬────────────┘                  ↓          MASTER CONTROL PLANE                  ↓     SUBSCRIPTIONS + FEATURE STORE                  ↓         SELL CAPABILITIES                  ↓           ORGANIZATIONS                  ↓            CONFIGURATION                  ↓              OPERATE                  ↓           DATA + EVENTS                  ↓        CRM / ANALYTICS / AI
40. Operating Principle
The complete Proviyaa model is: Build Once → Register Once → Release Once → Package or sell individually → Allow eligible organizations to subscribe → Create entitlement → Configure per organization → Activate in the appropriate experience → Measure usage and outcomes → Feed intelligence back into the platform.
This structure keeps the Master Control Plane as the central governance layer, Commerce OS as the shared transactional core, Vinii Store as the capability and commercial catalogue, Subscription Management as the commercial lifecycle system, and VINII POS / One Latur / OnLatur as configurable experience layers.
